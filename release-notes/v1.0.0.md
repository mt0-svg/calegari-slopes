The slopes of the Atkin operator U_3 on 3-adic overconvergent cusp forms of tame level 1 and weight 0 are the numbers 2i + 2v_3((2i)!/i!), i ≥ 1, each with multiplicity one, as Calegari conjectured.

This is AI-generated research: the results, proofs and code were found and written by AI. Credit goes to all the humans whose work it builds on.

**Proved in Lean 4** with Mathlib and only the standard axioms are the main theorem `CalegariSlopes.Final.calegari_conjecture_overconvergent`, the valuations of the coefficients of the characteristic series of the matrix of U_3 (`CalegariSlopes.Newton.calegari_conjecture`), the matrix form of Loeffler's conjecture on the factorization at other radii, with its exact intervals, and the spectral expansion of those matrices (Section 8, namespace `CalegariSlopes.Conj5`). Comparator with nanoda checks them on GitHub's runners against `CalegariSlopes/Challenge*.lean`, and the Lean kernel checks the table of Lemma 5.3, which `code/utab/gen_utab.gp` wrote. **By computation**, the PARI/GP scripts of `code/gp/` rerun the numbers of the paper that no Lean declaration states.

Assets: the paper, `verification-v1.0.0.tar.gz` (the complete output of every check of the CI run), and `calegari-slopes-x86_64-unknown-linux-gnu.tar.gz`, the Lake build that Comparator checked, which `lake build :release` and dependent packages download.
